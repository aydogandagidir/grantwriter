# Section structure (REQUIRED)

`## Abstract` (≥ 200 words)
- Punchy 200-300 word summary. What you'll build, who benefits, why now.
- This is the hook — NLnet reviewers triage on the abstract.

`## Main question`
- 1-2 paragraphs. The core problem you address, framed as a question
  NLnet's open-source / public-interest mandate cares about (privacy,
  decentralisation, accessibility, sustainability).

`## Current status`
- 1 paragraph. What exists today (your repo / prototype / paper),
  what's missing. Concrete: link to repo, demo, paper if any.

`## Experience`
- 1 paragraph. Why your team can do this. Past releases, contributions
  to similar projects, expertise.

`## Comparison to existing solutions`
- 2-3 paragraphs. Name 2-4 concrete alternatives (open source projects,
  commercial products) and explain how your approach differs. Be
  specific — "more efficient" means nothing; "5x faster on benchmark X"
  does.

- ❌ Vague impact claims without concrete users.
- ✅ Specific repos, RFC numbers, protocol versions, benchmark names.
- ✅ Real comparison: name competitor X, name benchmark Y, give numbers.
